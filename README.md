# Deepfakes and Cybersecurity

**Exploring the dual role of artificial intelligence as both a threat and a solution**

Research project for my HND in Cloud Computing (Unit 12), graded Distinction. 128 pages, 160+ sources, plus a primary survey of 30 people.

[Read the full report (PDF)](Deepfakes-and-Cybersecurity.pdf)

## Research question

How have deepfakes evolved into a major cybersecurity threat, and can artificial intelligence provide the solution to combating them?

## What it covers

1. **Foundations:** what deepfakes, AI and cybersecurity mean, and how they connect
2. **How deepfakes work:** autoencoders, GANs and diffusion models, and why detection is hard
3. **The threat:** case studies including the 2019 UK CEO voice-cloning fraud, the 2024 Biden robocalls and deepfake job interviews
4. **AI as a defence:** spatial, temporal and physiological detection methods, and tools such as Deepware Scanner
5. **Authentication:** C2PA Content Credentials, watermarking and provenance
6. **Limits of AI defences:** the generator vs detector arms race, generalisation failures and bias
7. **Ethics and policy:** privacy vs security, free speech vs regulation, the EU AI Act, US and Chinese law
8. **The future (2030 to 2040):** scenarios, including post-quantum cryptography
9. **Recommendations** for governments, organisations and individuals

## Survey findings

A questionnaire of 30 participants (mostly 18 to 24, many studying computing):

- **30%** didn't know deepfake detection tools exist, even among tech students
- **Only 13%** had ever used a tool to check whether media was AI-generated
- **67%** said creating deepfakes without consent should always be a criminal offence
- **63%** preferred a mix of solutions (technology, law and education) over any single fix

## Conclusion

AI is currently more of a threat than a solution when it comes to deepfakes. Detection alone loses the arms race. The balance can shift through a combination of content provenance standards like C2PA, harm-based legislation and public awareness.
